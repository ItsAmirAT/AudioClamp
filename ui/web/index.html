📖 Settings & Audio Dynamics Guide Field Manual
A detailed breakdown of every control, what it does, and how it impacts footsteps, gunshots, and game audio.
⚡ Peak Limiter (Master Toggle) LIVE: ONLINE
Master Limiting
WHAT IT IS

The primary real-time acoustic protection engine. Monitors audio output at 333 Hz with sub-millisecond lookahead to prevent hearing fatigue and acoustic trauma.

HOW IT AFFECTS GAME AUDIO

When ONLINE, AudioClamp transparently monitors incoming sound and applies smooth soft-knee attenuation the moment sound crosses your Loudness Ceiling. When OFF, audio passes completely unaltered in bit-perfect hardware bypass.

PRO TIP: Keep Online while playing competitive games so you can comfortably increase Windows master volume to hear faint distant steps without risking hearing damage during sudden point-blank shotgun blasts.
🎚️ Loudness Ceiling (Threshold dB) LIVE: -12.0 dB
Master Limiting
WHAT IT IS

The absolute maximum volume ceiling audio is permitted to reach. Measured in negative decibels full-scale (dBFS), where 0 dB is 100% maximum audio output.

HOW IT AFFECTS GAME AUDIO

All sounds below the ceiling (quiet enemy footsteps, crawling in grass, distant reloads, far engines) play at full target volume without any reduction. Any loud burst (AWP sniper, grenade explosion, full-auto rifle burst) is clamped down to this ceiling.

SWEET SPOT: -12 dB to -14 dB for high dynamic range games like PUBG and Tarkov. -8 dB to -10 dB for CS2 and Valorant. Use quick pills (-6, -12, -18, -24) or [Ctrl+Alt+↑/↓] hotkeys to nudge in real-time.
📈 Dynamics Mode (Soft-Knee vs Brickwall) LIVE: Soft-Knee (4:1)
Dynamics
WHAT IT IS

The compression curve applied as incoming audio approaches and passes above your Loudness Ceiling.

HOW IT AFFECTS GAME AUDIO

Soft-Knee (4:1): Progressively curves gain reduction around the threshold. Crucial for distance perception—allows you to distinguish whether an enemy gunshot was 25 meters away or 150 meters away because louder shots still retain subtle dynamic contrast.
Brickwall (∞:1): Applies a hard flat ceiling with zero overshoot. Maximizes ear protection against ear-piercing flashbangs, but flattens distance perception.

RECOMMENDATION: Always use Soft-Knee (4:1) for tactical shooters (PUBG, CS2, Tarkov, Warzone). Use Brickwall for late-night movie watching or extreme hearing sensitivity.
⏱️ Footstep Recovery (Release Time) LIVE: 140 ms
Dynamics
WHAT IT IS

The exponential decay rate (measured in milliseconds) at which volume returns to 100% normal level after a loud gunshot or explosion ceases.

HOW IT AFFECTS GAME AUDIO & ECHOES

If release is too fast (<80ms): Gunshots in open terrain or indoor rooms have natural reverberation tails. Recovering too quickly amplifies the reverb tail as it decays, causing unnatural echo surges or pumping sounds.
If release is too slow (>400ms): Footsteps immediately after a firefight remain quiet for too long.

ANTI-ECHO TUNING: Set between 120 ms and 150 ms. This ensures room reverberation decays naturally without echo pumping, while restoring 100% footstep audibility immediately after gunfire finishes.
🎯 Clamping Scope (Auto-Detect / Specific App / Master PC) LIVE: Auto-Detect Game
Routing & Scope
WHAT IT IS

Controls which audio sessions are clamped by the audio limiter engine.

HOW IT AFFECTS DISCORD & GAME AUDIO

Auto-Detect Game: Automatically attaches to any running game from your monitored games list (PUBG, CS2, Tarkov, Warzone, Apex, Valorant). Discord, Teamspeak, and Spotify remain completely uncompressed and clear.
Specific App: Locks onto an exact `.exe` of your choice via the drawer or Browse button. Only that game is clamped.
Master PC: Clamps the global audio endpoint for the entire computer.

DISCORD PROTECTION: Keep on Auto-Detect Game or Specific App so your teammates' voices never get quieted when you shoot your weapon.
👁️ Auto-Watch Mode LIVE: Active
Automation
WHAT IT IS

Intelligent process watcher that handles engine activation and volume restoration automatically in the background.

HOW IT AFFECTS SYSTEM RESOURCES

While you browse the web or work, AudioClamp sleeps at virtually 0.0% CPU with volume at 100%. The moment your game opens, it activates in under 0.4 seconds. When you close the game, it instantly restores all volumes to 100% and returns to standby.

HANDS-FREE: Keep enabled so you never need to remember to open or close AudioClamp before playing.
🏆 Competitive Profiles & Auto-Switching LIVE: PUBG
Profiles
WHAT IT IS

Custom acoustic profiles calibrated for the sound engines of specific competitive shooters.

HOW IT AFFECTS GAMEPLAY

• PUBG: -12 dB ceiling, 130ms recovery — suppresses loud Beryl/M416/redzone spikes while keeping footsteps and distant vehicle engines audible.
• CS2: -8 dB ceiling, 80ms recovery — fast recovery for rapid AWP shots, AK-47 sprays, and bomb defusal audio.
• Tarkov: -10 dB ceiling, 120ms recovery — heavy blast concussive absorption with crisp headset perception.
• Warzone / CoD: -14 dB ceiling, 150ms recovery — tames precision airstrikes, cluster mines, and vehicle horns.
• Cinema / Late Night: Deep ceilings (-15 to -20 dB) with long releases for dialogue leveling.

AUTO-SWITCHING: AudioClamp monitors your active foreground game window and automatically applies the matching profile when you focus the game.
🍃 Eco Mode (Ultra-Low CPU) LIVE: Off
Power & Resources
WHAT IT IS

Low-power battery saver mode designed for gaming laptops and low-spec systems.

HOW IT AFFECTS SYSTEM PERFORMANCE

Scales active WASAPI polling from 3.0 ms to 6.0 ms, relaxes idle silent polling to 35 ms, and caps UI visualizer rendering to 30 FPS. Audio limiting remains active and accurate while CPU cycles and battery draw are significantly reduced.
